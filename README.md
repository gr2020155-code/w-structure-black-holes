## Abstract

In the W-Structure, a black hole is the saturation limit 3W: all possible links are realized and nothing changes inside. There is no singularity. Outside, space densifies layer by layer: the clock rate is e^(−δ) and the number of steps per unit path is e^(+δ). With δ = GM/(rc²) this is the exponential metric (as in Papapetrou and Yilmaz), which differs from general relativity: the throat is at e·GM/c² ≈ 2.72 GM/c², and the shadow radius is 2e·GM/c² ≈ 5.44 GM/c², 4.6 % larger than in GR (for non-rotating layers and the unmodified exponential metric near the photon orbit). What is new is the derivation of the field form from layers and links, and what lies beyond the throat; the numbers for this metric are known.

**Predictions:** a 4.6 % larger shadow (for the unmodified exponential metric); a finite redshift of light emitted near the throat; no black holes lighter than the neutron-star limit (minus mass carried away in collapse); no echoes; no steady Hawking flux, because there is no horizon and the redshift at the throat is finite. Every point that is not derived is listed in section 9, "Open questions".

## Changes in version 1.1

- The shadow prediction (4.6 %) is now stated as a prediction of the unmodified exponential metric: the photon orbit lies at δ = 1/2, where the field law is not tested, and a third-order change that keeps the perihelion and light deflection can still shift the shadow (section 4).
- The absence of Hawking radiation is now argued from the absence of a horizon and the finite redshift at the throat (no exponential stretching of light rays), not from "nothing to radiate". A burst during formation and the effect of the absorbing throat on quantum fields are listed as open (section 6, open question 14).
