## Abstract

In the W-Structure, a black hole is the saturation limit 3W: all possible links are realized and nothing changes inside. There is no singularity. Outside, space densifies layer by layer: the clock rate is e^(−δ) and the number of steps per unit path is e^(+δ). With δ = GM/(rc²) this is the exponential metric (as in Papapetrou and Yilmaz), which differs from general relativity: the throat is at e·GM/c² ≈ 2.72 GM/c², and the shadow radius is 2e·GM/c² ≈ 5.44 GM/c², 4.6 % larger than in GR (for non-rotating layers). What is new is the derivation of the field form from layers and links, and what lies beyond the throat; the numbers for this metric are known.

**Predictions:** a 4.6 % larger shadow; a finite redshift of light emitted near the throat; no black holes lighter than the neutron-star limit (minus mass carried away in collapse); no echoes and no Hawking radiation from 3W. Every point that is not derived is listed in section 9, "Open questions".
